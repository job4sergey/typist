On a quiet Sunday afternoon, Peter decided to repair an old wooden chair that had been standing in his garage for several years. He carefully examined the damaged legs, collected the necessary tools, and began working near the open window. After replacing two screws, he discovered a small crack beneath the seat. Instead of ignoring it, Peter searched for some glue and repaired the damaged section. When the chair was finally ready, he carried it into the garden and sat down to enjoy the evening sunshine.
