On Thursday afternoon, a technician arrived at the museum to repair a faulty security camera. The manager explained that the device had stopped recording during the previous evening. After examining the cables, the technician discovered a damaged connector behind a narrow wooden panel. Replacing it was straightforward, but reaching the equipment required moving several heavy cabinets. Once everything was working again, the manager checked the recordings, thanked the technician, and returned to preparing the exhibition for the following morning.
