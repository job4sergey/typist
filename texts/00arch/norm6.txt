The train arrived a few minutes later than expected, so most passengers
were already waiting near the edge of the platform. A woman carrying two
large bags checked the number on her ticket and walked toward the opposite
end. Near the stairs, a family was discussing whether they had enough time
to buy something to drink. When the doors finally opened, people stepped
aside to let several passengers leave before moving inside. I found an empty
seat beside the window and put my backpack on the floor. Outside, warehouses
and apartment buildings slowly disappeared as the train gathered speed.
After twenty minutes the city was behind us, and fields, narrow roads, and
small villages began to appear between patches of forest.
