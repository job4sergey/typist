On some days everything seems to happen in the right order. A task
that looked difficult in the morning becomes simple once you begin,
and several small problems disappear before they require much
attention. On other days even familiar work can feel surprisingly
slow. You may read the same sentence twice, forget what you were
about to do, or spend too much time making an easy decision. These
changes are normal. Concentration is not perfectly constant, so a
useful routine should work even when attention is weaker than usual.
The best approach is often to continue at a comfortable pace, finish
one thing at a time, and avoid turning a small mistake into a larger
interruption.
