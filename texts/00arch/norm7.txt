Several customers gathered near the information desk while an employee
searched through a collection of documents. Someone had accidentally
placed a reservation under the wrong address, and another person was
trying to exchange a damaged purchase. The receptionist answered each
question carefully and entered the information into the computer.
Meanwhile, people continued arriving through the entrance, carrying
packages, umbrellas, backpacks, and shopping bags. Eventually the line
became shorter, and everyone moved forward without needing to hurry.
